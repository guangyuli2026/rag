Campus Weather Notes (fictional sample)

> This file is a fictional sample for the RAG learning project only. It does not describe any real place or policy.

## Spring

Spring on campus is warm and windy. Students usually wear a light jacket from March to May. The cherry trees near the library bloom in early April, which is the most popular photo spot.

## Summer

Summer is hot and humid. The swimming pool opens from June to September. Classrooms are air-conditioned, so bring a thin sweater even in July.

## Autumn

Autumn is the most comfortable season. The temperature stays between 15 and 25 degrees from September to November. Many students choose this season for outdoor sports and weekend trips.

## Winter

Winter is dry and cold, with occasional snow in January. The heating system starts in mid-November. Wearing layers is the best strategy against the cold.

## General advice

Always check the daily forecast before planning outdoor activities. Rain can come suddenly in spring, so an umbrella is a good companion all year round.
